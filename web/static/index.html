Overall Engagement
The combined neural engagement score across all five brain regions. Higher values mean the brain is processing more of the video content at that moment. Look for peaks (high-attention moments) and troughs (where viewers may disengage).
FFA — Faces
Activation of the Fusiform Face Area — the brain region specialised for face recognition. Spikes when faces appear on screen. Strong face activation typically correlates with higher emotional engagement and memorability.
EBA — Bodies
Activation of the Extrastriate Body Area — responds to human bodies, hands, and gestures. Sustained elevation means people are consistently on screen. Drops may indicate product-only shots or environment-focused moments.
PPA — Scenes
Activation of the Parahippocampal Place Area — responds to environments, landscapes, and spatial scenes. High when the video shows wide establishing shots, interiors, or location context. Low during close-ups on people or products.
STS — Social Cues
Activation of the Superior Temporal Sulcus — responds to social signals like eye gaze, facial expressions, and biological motion. High during interpersonal moments, dialogue, and emotionally charged interactions between people.
Auditory Cortex — Audio Contribution
The solid line shows auditory cortex activation with the full audio track; the dashed line shows activation with audio stripped. The shaded gap between them is the audio's independent contribution. Large gaps mean the soundtrack is doing real work — important for assessing impact on muted social media placements.
