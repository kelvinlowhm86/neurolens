NeuroLens privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

NeuroLens is a university course project. It predicts the brain response to a video and shows the main brain response chart, the strongest and weakest moments, and a 3D brain map. It is not a commercial service. This page says what data it handles.

What we collect

  • Your email address, from Google or from the address you sign up with. We use it only to recognise you when you come back and to tell the team who is who.
  • Videos you upload, and the analysis results made from them, including the 3D brain data.
  • A job record for each upload: the file name, when it ran, how long the video was and what it cost in credit.
  • Your consent choice for each upload: whether you agreed to keep it for future research. The box is unticked by default and the analysis works either way.
  • A real audience-retention report, if you choose to add one to a video you consented to keep.

The 3D brain data is part of the analysis result and, for a video you consent to keep, of the dataset copy. Deleting a video removes both.

We do not receive your Google password or your contacts. If you sign in with Google, we receive only your email address and whether Google has verified it.

How long we keep it

  • Uploaded videos that are not kept for research are deleted automatically within 3 days (the 2-day rule, rounded to midnight UTC). This includes an upload that arrives after its job was refunded: it is removed only by that 2-day rule.
  • Results are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • A video you consented to keep, its analysis and any real audience-retention report you add are kept for up to 12 months, then deleted automatically. They are used only to help build a future model linking brain response to real audience behaviour.
  • Your email address and job records stay until you delete your account, or until the project is taken down.

Consent for research

Each upload has a separate, optional consent box. If you tick it, you confirm that you own the rights to the video and agree that NeuroLens may keep the video and its analysis for up to 12 months to help build a future model linking brain response to real audience behaviour. If you leave it unticked, your video is analysed exactly the same way and is not kept beyond the normal retention periods above.

Who can see it

Only you, and the NeuroLens team, can see your videos and results. We do not sell or share them, and we do not use them for advertising. Everything is stored with Amazon Web Services in the United States (us-east-1). Sign-in is handled by Amazon Cognito.

Deleting your data

You can delete any analysis from your history on the main page: this removes its video, result (including the 3D brain data) and dataset files. You can also delete your whole account there by typing DELETE to confirm; this removes every video, analysis and row we hold, and your NeuroLens login. A deletion is refused while one of your videos is still queued or processing. To ask what we hold about you, email the team at the contact address shown on the Google sign-in screen.
