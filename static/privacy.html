NeuroLens privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

NeuroLens is a university course project. It predicts how a video engages different brain regions and shows the result as charts. It is not a commercial service. This page says what data it handles.

What we collect

  • Your email address, from Google or from the address you sign up with. We use it only to recognise you when you come back and to tell the team who is who.
  • Videos you upload, and the analysis results made from them.
  • A job record for each upload: the file name, when it ran, how long the video was and what it cost in credit.

We do not receive your Google password or your contacts. If you sign in with Google, we receive only your email address and whether Google has verified it.

How long we keep it

  • Uploaded videos are deleted automatically after 2 days.
  • Results are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Your email address and job records stay until the project is taken down, or until you ask for them to be deleted.

Who can see it

Only you, and the NeuroLens team, can see your videos and results. We do not sell or share them, and we do not use them for advertising. Everything is stored with Amazon Web Services in the United States (us-east-1). Sign-in is handled by Amazon Cognito.

Payments

NeuroLens takes no real payments. Where a team member can add test credit, it uses Stripe's test mode: no real card is charged and we store no card details.

Asking about or deleting your data

To ask what we hold about you, or to have your account and uploads deleted, email the team at the contact address shown on the Google sign-in screen.
