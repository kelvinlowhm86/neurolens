NeuroLens privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

NeuroLens is an app that predicts the brain response to a video and shows the main brain response chart, a written report and a 3D brain map. Analysing your own videos uses credit from the NeuroLens team; the sample videos are free. Videos that people choose to share may later help build NeuroLens's own models, which may become a paid product — that part is described under "Sharing a video" below. This page says what data NeuroLens handles.

What we collect

  • Your email address, from Google or from the address you sign up with. We use it only to recognise your account when you sign in and when you come back.
  • Videos you upload, and the analysis results made from them, including the 3D brain data. We also keep a copy re-encoded to play on phones; it is deleted with the video, kept with the result (30 days), and is not part of any shared copy.
  • A job record for each upload: the file name, when it ran, how long the video was and what it cost in credit.
  • A small picture from each video (its thumbnail, shown in History). It is kept with the result and is not part of any shared copy.
  • Your sharing choice for each upload: whether you agreed to share it (below). The box is unticked by default and the analysis works either way.
  • A real audience-retention curve, if you choose to add one to a video you share, together with the details the form asks for (whether it was promoted with ads, whether it was re-cut, and the video's link). When we can check the video, we also keep its publish date, length and view count.

The 3D brain data is part of the analysis result. Deleting a video removes it.

We do not receive your Google password or your contacts. If you sign in with Google, we receive only your email address and whether Google has verified it.

How long we keep it

  • Uploaded videos that are not shared are deleted automatically after 30 days (up to 31 days, since deletion happens overnight). They are deleted at once when the job is cancelled or refunded.
  • Results are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • A video you share, and any retention curve you add, are kept for up to 3 years. If no curve is added, the shared copy is deleted 90 days after upload.
  • Your email address and job records stay until you delete your account, or until the project is taken down.

Sharing a video

Each upload has a separate, optional box: "Share to help improve NeuroLens. I own the rights to it." Sharing is never required and changes nothing about the analysis — the same prediction runs either way.

If you share a video, we keep the video itself, its file details, and any audience-retention curve you add from YouTube or Facebook, with the details given alongside it. When we can check the video, we also keep its publish date, length and view count. We use them to help improve NeuroLens, by building and testing its own engagement models, which may later be offered as a paid product — with notice first. The models are trained on videos and real audience data, never on NeuroLens's brain predictions. Nothing is published, sold or given outside the team; anything shown publicly is only statistics. Everything is stored with Amazon Web Services in the United States.

The shared copy is kept for up to 3 years, or deleted 90 days after upload if no curve was added. "Stop sharing" in the video's menu, deleting the video, or deleting your account removes the shared copy at once. A model trained before that is not retrained, but your data is not used again.

By sharing, you confirm that you own the rights to the video and that any identifiable people in it agreed to appear. You keep all rights to the video and give us a free, non-exclusive licence to use it for the purposes above. As a thank-you, a valid audience-retention curve added to a video that has been public at least 7 days (7 days after the analysis when we can't check the publish date) can make that video's analysis free: its price comes back as credit (not cash). When we can check it, the video must be the same length as the analysed video, give or take 2 seconds. This applies once per published video (the same file or link counts once), to up to 5 videos in any 30 days, and only on accounts that have had credit added. A curve counts as valid when it has at least 20 points, is not flat, does not rise overall and never goes above 1,000%.

AI reports

When you ask for a report, the video's frames (one per second), its spoken words and its brain numbers are sent to Claude on Amazon Bedrock, processed in AWS's US regions. AWS states that Bedrock does not store them or use them for training. The frames and the report are deleted with the job, and expire with the result after 30 days. When you ask for a comparison, the same things for both videos are sent the same way; the comparison is deleted when either video is deleted, and expires after 30 days.

Who can see it

Only you, and the NeuroLens team, can see your videos and results. We do not sell or share them, and we do not use them for advertising. Sign-in is handled by Amazon Cognito.

Deleting your data

You can delete any analysis from your history on the main page: this removes its video, result (including the 3D brain data) and any shared copy. You can also delete your whole account there by typing DELETE to confirm; this removes every video, analysis and row we hold, and your NeuroLens login. If a video is still queued or processing, wait for it to finish, then try deleting your account again. To ask what we hold about you, email joshualumzy@gmail.com.
