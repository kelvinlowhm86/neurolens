How to get your audience-retention report

Thank you for sharing your audience-retention report. Your contribution helps us understand how people watch videos and improve NeuroLens. Follow the steps below to get the report from YouTube or Facebook and add it to the analysis of a finished video you've shared.

When the link is one we can check, the "Add audience retention" window fills in the video's own values and shows the amount you can receive before you do any work.

YouTube

  1. Open Google's APIs Explorer for the YouTube Analytics API method reports.query: https://developers.google.com/youtube/analytics/reference/reports/query. The "Try it" section on that page opens the APIs Explorer.
  2. Sign in with the Google account that owns the channel, and allow read-only access when Google asks.
  3. Fill in these values:
    • ids: channel==MINE
    • startDate: the day before the video was published, as YYYY-MM-DD
    • endDate: today, as YYYY-MM-DD
    • metrics: audienceWatchRatio,relativeRetentionPerformance
    • dimensions: elapsedVideoTimeRatio
    • filters: video== followed by the video's ID (the part after v= in its link)
  4. Run it.
  5. Copy the whole JSON response, including the opening { and closing }.
  6. In NeuroLens, click "Add audience retention" on the finished video's row or result, paste the reply into the "Paste the reply here" box (or use "or choose a file"), fill in the remaining details and click "Save".

Add it once the video has been public a week to get its credit back.

Facebook

  1. Open Meta's Graph API Explorer: https://developers.facebook.com/tools/explorer/.
  2. Under User or Page, pick the Page that published the video, and allow the pages_read_engagement and read_insights permissions when Meta asks.
  3. Paste this into the query field, then click Submit: <video id>/video_insights?metric=total_video_retention_graph .
  4. Copy the whole JSON reply, including the opening { and closing }.
  5. In NeuroLens, click "Add audience retention" on the finished video's row or result, paste the reply into the "Paste the reply here" box (or use "or choose a file"), paste the video's Facebook link, fill in the remaining details and click "Save".

Add it once the video has been public a week to get its credit back.

If your report is empty

A response with no rows means YouTube has no retention data available for the video yet. This can happen with new videos or ones with very few views.

Check that the video ID is correct and the date range runs from the video's publication date to today. If those are correct, try getting the report again later, once YouTube has more data. You can still view your NeuroLens analysis without adding a report.

Written from the YouTube Analytics API and Meta Graph API documentation as of 2 November 2026; Google and Meta may change their tools.
