How to get your audience-retention report

Thank you for sharing your audience-retention report. Your contribution helps us understand how people watch videos and improve NeuroLens. Follow the steps below to get the report from YouTube and add it to the analysis of a finished video you've shared for research.

If your analysis is eligible for a credit refund, the "Add audience retention" window will show the amount you can receive.

Get the report

  1. Open Google's APIs Explorer for the YouTube Analytics API method reports.query: https://developers.google.com/youtube/analytics/reference/reports/query. The "Try it" section on that page opens the APIs Explorer.
  2. Sign in with the Google account that owns the channel, and allow read-only access when Google asks.
  3. Fill in these values:
    • ids: channel==MINE
    • startDate: the day the video was published, as YYYY-MM-DD
    • endDate: today, as YYYY-MM-DD
    • metrics: audienceWatchRatio,relativeRetentionPerformance
    • dimensions: elapsedVideoTimeRatio
    • filters: video== followed by the video's ID (the part after v= in its link)
  4. Run it.
  5. Copy the whole JSON response, including the opening { and closing }. Paste it into a plain-text editor and save it as retention.json. Make sure the filename ends in .json, not .txt.
  6. In NeuroLens, click "Add audience retention" on the finished video's row or result, choose your retention.json file, fill in the remaining details and click "Save".

If your report is empty

A response with no rows means YouTube has no retention data available for the video yet. This can happen with new videos or ones with very few views.

Written from the YouTube Analytics API documentation as of 15 October 2026; Google may change its tools.
