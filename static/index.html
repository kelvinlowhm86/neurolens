How it works

  1. Upload a clip

    Any video up to the upload limit. Sample videos are free to explore without an account.

  2. A brain model predicts the response

    TRIBE v2, a research model from Meta trained on brain scans of people watching films, estimates activity at 20,484 points across the brain for every second. A result takes about 10 minutes.

  3. Read the report

    Curves for seven brain areas, the strongest and weakest moments, and a 3D brain that replays with your video.
